# BR-01 — Visual Direction Decision Matrix

Status: PROPOSED
Round: 02

## Direction NL-X1 — Dark Mineral Laboratory
This is the current lead direction.

### Foundation
Near-black mineral space, engineered dark materials, precision typography and restrained spectral signals.

### 3D signature
Procedural computational matter organized around layered cores and information flows.

### Distinctiveness strategy
Own the combination of:
- physical-looking computational structures;
- semantic system motion;
- extreme UI restraint;
- recurring NexLabs geometry grammar.

### What it must NOT become
- gaming website;
- crypto protocol;
- generic AI landing page;
- VFX showreel;
- luxury fashion site;
- spaceship dashboard.

## Experience rhythm

### Moment 01 — Signal
Fast visual recognition. Logo/name/headline are immediately available while 3D initializes independently.

### Moment 02 — Structure
The Context Core reveals enough behavior to create curiosity.

### Moment 03 — Meaning
Copy and labels explain the actual infrastructure problem and HIVE.

### Moment 04 — Evidence
Open-source repository, product capabilities and technical proof replace abstract claims.

### Moment 05 — Company
NexLabs thesis expands beyond the first product without pretending future products already exist.

### Moment 06 — Action
GitHub, product exploration and contact become obvious endpoints.

## Proposed homepage visual cadence
Hero: strongest 3D scene.
Problem: mostly typography + subtle field behavior.
HIVE: interactive technical 3D/diagram hybrid.
Technology: modular visual components.
Open Source/Evidence: quiet, factual, minimal animation.
About: company thesis.
Footer: restrained motion signature.

This cadence prevents every section from competing for GPU and attention.

## Preliminary logo implication
The final logo should NOT literally depict the full Context Core. It should derive from the same geometry grammar so it remains simple at favicon scale.

Possible construction families for BR-03:
1. nested N / spatial layers;
2. node-to-structure transformation;
3. modular core/gateway;
4. negative-space N formed by routing paths;
5. abstract topology mark with a hidden N.

No concept is selected yet.

## Approval recommendation
Advance NL-X1 as the lead art direction into BR-02/03/04 while retaining the right to reject specific logo/color executions during visual testing.
