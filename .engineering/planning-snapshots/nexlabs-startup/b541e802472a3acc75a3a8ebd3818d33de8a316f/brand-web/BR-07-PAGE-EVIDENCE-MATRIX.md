## Evidence tags for content planning
[E-COMPANY] STARTUP-MASTER / company profile
[E-HIVE] HIVE repository/docs
[E-OSS] public license/repository evidence
[E-FOUNDER] founder-approved public facts
[E-RUNTIME] actual production analytics/forms/cookies behavior

## Content rule
Every externally factual paragraph should be traceable to one or more evidence tags during drafting. Creative headlines may express positioning, but cannot contradict evidence.
