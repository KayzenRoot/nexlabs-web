# BR-03 — NexLabs Logo System

Status: CONCEPT ARCHITECTURE
Date: 2026-09-19
Implementation: deferred to Codex + Blender MCP after visual freeze

## 1. Objective
Create a proprietary, technically credible mark that remains recognizable at favicon scale while belonging naturally to the same geometric world as NexLabs' 3D computational-matter system.

The mark must work before animation, without gradients and without 3D.

## 2. Core design principle — Projection Identity
The 2D NexLabs symbol should be derivable from, or strongly consistent with, a canonical 3D structure.

Relationship:
3D canonical structure → controlled projection/silhouette → 2D master mark → responsive simplifications.

This makes motion/3D a reveal of the identity rather than decoration added after the logo.

## 3. Non-negotiable tests
Every candidate must pass:
- 16 px favicon recognition;
- 24/32 px UI icon;
- monochrome black;
- monochrome white;
- single-color print;
- light background;
- dark background;
- no-gradient version;
- blurred/squint silhouette test;
- horizontal wordmark lockup;
- square avatar;
- static reduced-motion state;
- simple geometry reproducible as SVG paths.

Failing a core test eliminates the candidate regardless of 3D beauty.

## 4. Construction grid
Initial exploration grid: 12 × 12 modular units.

The grid is a construction aid, not a visible aesthetic.
Candidate geometry should prefer:
- few dominant strokes/masses;
- controlled radii;
- repeatable angles;
- intentional negative space;
- optical correction after mathematical construction.

Avoid microscopic gaps that collapse at small sizes.

## 5. Candidate families

### NX-A — Nested N
An N emerges from two or more spatial layers/shells.

2D:
A compact N-like topology with an internal layer/gap suggesting depth.

3D:
Nested structural planes or shells align into the mark from the canonical camera.

Strengths:
- immediate brand initial;
- strong projection story;
- easy relationship to Context Core.

Risks:
- can resemble generic tech monograms;
- excess layers hurt small-size readability.

### NX-B — Routed N
The N exists primarily in negative space between controlled routing paths.

2D:
Solid structural masses define an N-shaped void.

3D:
Data/signal routes move through a structure; camera alignment reveals the N.

Strengths:
- “infrastructure/routing” meaning;
- elegant negative-space opportunity;
- animation can explain the mark.

Risks:
- negative space can disappear at 16 px;
- route imagery can become circuit-board cliché.

### NX-C — Modular Core
A compact abstract core/gateway based on modular cells; the N is secondary/hidden.

2D:
Ownable abstract symbol, not obviously a letter at first glance.

3D:
Modules separate, route information and resolve into the core.

Strengths:
- broad company identity beyond HIVE;
- potentially most ownable;
- excellent motion system.

Risks:
- weaker immediate association with NexLabs;
- requires stronger launch repetition to build recognition.

### NX-D — Topological N
A continuous topological surface/path folds into an N-like projection.

2D:
Minimal continuous form.

3D:
A single computational surface/path changes depth while retaining structural continuity.

Strengths:
- elegant 2D↔3D relationship;
- “continuity/context” semantic fit;
- potentially premium and distinctive.

Risks:
- easy to overcomplicate;
- may resemble existing ribbon/infinity marks if insufficiently original.

### NX-E — Node-to-Structure
Sparse nodes resolve into a precise structural N/core.

2D:
Only the resolved structural state becomes the master mark; nodes are motion language, not permanent clutter.

3D/motion:
Distributed data matter converges into the identity.

Strengths:
- strongest transformation narrative;
- excellent logo reveal.

Risks:
- static resolved symbol still needs a unique geometry independent of particles.

## 6. Current shortlist recommendation
Explore three families deeply:
1. **NX-D Topological N**
2. **NX-C Modular Core**
3. **NX-B Routed N**

NX-A remains a control candidate.
NX-E becomes a motion behavior applicable to the winner rather than a separate static-logo family.

No winner is selected in planning without visual comparison.

## 7. Wordmark architecture
Primary wordmark text: **NexLabs**
Institutional extension: **Technology**

Recommended hierarchy:
NexLabs [primary]
Technology [optional secondary descriptor]

Do not force “Technology” into favicon or compact mark.

## 8. Typographic direction for wordmark
Custom or modified geometric/neo-grotesk construction preferred over a stereotypical sci-fi display font.

Desired:
- highly legible;
- technical precision;
- subtle custom details;
- strong lowercase/uppercase behavior;
- international readability.

Avoid:
- segmented “digital” fonts;
- excessive extended width;
- unreadable futuristic cuts;
- copying recognizable competitor wordmarks.

Exact typeface selected in BR-04 with licensing verified for zero-cost commercial use.

## 9. Responsive logo set
L0 — canonical symbol.
L1 — symbol + NexLabs.
L2 — symbol + NexLabs + Technology descriptor.
L3 — micro/favicon simplification if required.
L4 — monochrome.
L5 — motion/3D identity.

## 10. Color independence
Geometry is approved in monochrome first.
Color/gradient may enhance but never rescue a weak mark.

## 11. 3D canonical object contract
The eventual Blender master object must:
- use clean named collections;
- preserve a canonical projection camera;
- allow material swapping;
- support a low-poly/web representation;
- expose animation-ready components;
- avoid destructive modeling where procedural construction is useful;
- export cleanly to the selected web asset pipeline.

Detailed Blender specification belongs to BR-11.

## 12. Motion-logo principle
Proposed behavior:
distributed structure/data → routing/alignment → topology resolves → canonical NexLabs mark → stable idle state.

Duration variants planned:
- micro: ~0.5–1 s;
- standard UI/site reveal: ~1.5–3 s;
- cinematic brand ident: ~4–7 s.

Exact timing is not frozen until motion tests.

## 13. Trademark/originality gate
Before final brand freeze:
- perform visual similarity research against relevant technology/AI marks;
- perform basic name/mark conflict research in target markets;
- do not treat design originality review as legal trademark clearance;
- preserve construction/source files and decision history.

## 14. Acceptance criteria
BR-03 can freeze only when:
- one primary family and one fallback are selected;
- 2D master passes all non-negotiable tests;
- wordmark hierarchy is approved;
- projection relationship is reproducible;
- favicon is readable;
- monochrome works;
- no obvious competitor imitation is identified;
- Blender/web implementation contract is feasible.
